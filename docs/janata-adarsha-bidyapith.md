# Janata Adarsha Bidyapith (জনতা আদর্শ বিদ্যাপীঠ)

> **"People's Model Institute of Learning"**

## Overview

Janata Adarsha Bidyapith is a non-government secondary school established on **18 March 1969** in Palash, Narsingdi, Bangladesh. Officially recognized on **1 January 1973**, the school has served the local community for over five decades as a center for accessible, affordable education. It operates under the Dhaka Education Board and is included in the government's Monthly Pay Order (MPO) system.

## Academic Profile

- Follows the **Bangladesh National Curriculum** under the Directorate of Secondary and Higher Education (DSHE).
- Offers three academic streams at the SSC level: **Science**, **Business Studies**, and **Humanities**.
- Students sit for the **SSC (Secondary School Certificate)** examination under the Dhaka Education Board.

---

## Role in the Community

Janata Adarsha Bidyapith serves as an important educational institution in the Palash upazila area. For over 55 years, the school has:

- Provided accessible and affordable secondary education to students from surrounding villages and communities.
- Contributed to improving literacy rates and creating pathways to higher education for local students.
- Hosted extracurricular activities including sports, cultural programs, and national day observances.
- Built a strong community-based reputation, with families choosing the school for its accessibility and consistent academic standards.
