### Key Observations

- **Founding headmaster Mijanur Rahman Chowdhury** served the longest tenure of nearly 19 years (1970–1989), establishing the school from its early days just one year after its founding in 1969.
- **Md. Shahabuddin** served the second-longest tenure of nearly 20 years (2000–2019). He initially took charge as Acting Headmaster in January 2000 and was confirmed as full Headmaster in January 2003.
- Two headmasters served in an **acting capacity** before either being confirmed or replaced: Babu Shurja Mohan Dutta (one month in 1989) and Md. Shahabuddin (three years, 2000–2002).
- The period from **1989 to 2000 saw rapid turnover** — four headmasters in roughly 11 years, with Abdul Wahab and Kazi Mohammad Razi Uddin each serving less than two years.
- All headmasters held at least a **Bachelor's degree plus a B.Ed.** (Bachelor of Education) teaching qualification, except M. A. Alim who held a law degree (L.L.B.) instead of B.Ed.
- The school has been led by **7 individuals across 55+ years** of operation, with two long-tenured headmasters (Chowdhury and Shahabuddin) accounting for roughly 39 of those years.
- **Md. Masud Khan** is the current and 7th headmaster, having taken charge in November 2019.
